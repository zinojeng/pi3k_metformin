## 1. ADA *Standards of Care in Diabetes—2026*：**確有專章處理 PI3Kα inhibitor 高血糖**

### 1.0 結論先行（重要更正）

**查證結果：ADA SOC—2026 並非「未見專章」。**
2026 年版**首次**新增了針對 PI3Kα inhibitor（明列 alpelisib 與 inavolisib）高血糖的**衛教、篩檢監測、預防、治療**四組建議，分別落在 Section 2、Section 3、Section 9。這是相對於既往版本的重大新增。

ADA 在文字上**明確把 PI3Kα inhibitor 與 ICI、mTOR inhibitor 分開處理**，三者的監測頻率與一線藥物建議**各不相同**——這點對本回顧的「不可把所有 PI3K/AKT inhibitors 當同一類」要求非常關鍵。

> **注意**：ADA 全篇使用的類別詞是 **PI3Kα inhibitor**（α isoform），並在 Rec 9.35a 明白寫成「PI3K inhibitors **that affect the α isoform**」。ADA **並未**把 idelalisib／duvelisib／copanlisib 納入這些建議。

---

### 1.1 Summary of Revisions 對新增條目的說明 [S4]

> Recommendation 2.19 was added to underscore the role of counseling and education regarding the risk of hyperglycemia in people initiating treatment with immune checkpoint inhibitors, phosphoinositidylinositol 3-kinase α (PI3Kα) inhibitors, and other anticancer therapy medications.

> Recommendation 2.21 was added to emphasize close monitoring of plasma glucose in people initiating treatment with PI3Kα inhibitors, which are associated with a particularly high risk of hyperglycemia during the first weeks of treatment.

> ...recommend considering using metformin to prevent hyperglycemia in high-risk individuals treated with a PI3Kα inhibitor (e.g., alpelisib and inavolisib).

> ...the need for initiation of insulin therapy due to the potential risk of diabetic ketoacidosis while additional testing is completed to determine if the hyperglycemia is related to immunotherapy-associated diabetes. For individuals with hyperglycemia due to mTOR inhibitors or PI3K inhibitors, Recommendations 9.34 and 9.35a state that metformin should be considered as first-line treatment, and Recommendation 9.35b states that insulin should be reserved for severe hyperglycemia and hyperglycemic crises due to its potential impact on PI3K inhibitor efficacy.

**證據等級標記：【L3】學會指引（ADA SOC 為系統性 evidence-graded 指引）；ADA 自身 grading 見各條末英文字母。**

> **2.19**（前段接續）...ple initiating treatment with immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs), including anti-PD-1 or anti-PDL-1 therapy (e.g., nivolumab, pembrolizumab, avelumab), phosphoinositidylinositol 3-kinase α (PI3Kα) inhibitors (e.g., alpelisib, inavolisib), or mammalian target of rapamycin (mTOR) inhibitors (e.g., everolimus), should be educated regarding risks, symptoms, and signs of hyperglycemia and hyperglycemic crises. **E**

> **2.20** In people treated with ICIs, fasting or random plasma glucose should be tested before initiating treatment, during each visit, or if symptoms and signs of hyperglycemia develop during or after treatment cessation. **E**

> **2.21** In people treated with PI3Kα inhibitors, fasting or random plasma glucose and A1C should be tested before initiating treatment, and random plasma glucose should be tested weekly for the first 2 weeks of treatment and then every 4 weeks during treatment. **C** Consider testing A1C every 3 months during treatment. **E**

> **2.22** In people treated with mTOR inhibitors, fasting or random plasma glucose should be tested before starting and at each visit throughout the duration of treatment. Consider testing A1C every 3 months during treatment. **C**

### 1.4 Section 3：**預防性 metformin** — Recommendations 3.7–3.10（逐字）[S2]

> **3.7** Metformin for the prevention of type 2 diabetes should be considered in adults at high risk of type 2 diabetes, as typified by the Diabetes Prevention Program, especially those aged 25–59 years with BMI ≥35 kg/m2, higher fasting plasma glucose (e.g., ≥110 mg/dL [≥6 mmol/L]), and higher A1C (e.g., ≥6.0% [≥42 mmol/mol]), and in individuals with prior gestational diabetes mellitus. **A**

> **3.8** Consider using metformin to prevent hyperglycemia in **high-risk individuals** treated with a phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase α (PI3Kα) inhibitor (e.g., alpelisib and inavolisib). **B**

> **3.9** Consider using metformin to prevent hyperglycemia in high-risk individuals treated with high-dose glucocorticoids. **B**

> **3.10** Consider periodic assessment of vitamin B12 levels in individuals receiving long-term metformin therapy, especially in those with anemia or peripheral neuropathy. **B**

> ⚠ **Rec 3.8 的措辭是 "Consider ... in high-risk individuals"，evidence grade 為 B，不是「所有病人都該用」。** ADA 在下方敘述段落自行定義了 high-risk 的四項因子（見 §1.5），並明白要求權衡 metformin 的腹瀉風險。撰寫回顧時**不得**把 METALLICA（單臂 phase 2）或 Rec 3.8 讀成全體適用。

> ...need for initiation of insulin therapy due to the potential risk of diabetic ketoacidosis while additional testing is completed to determine if the hyperglycemia is related to immunotherapy-associated diabetes. Close monitoring, education, and dose adjustment are needed if insulin is started. **C**

> **9.34** Consider metformin as the first-line treatment of hyperglycemia due to mTOR inhibitors. **E**

> **9.35a** Consider metformin as the first-line treatment of hyperglycemia due to phosphoinositide 3-kinase (PI3K) inhibitors **that affect the α isoform** (e.g., alpelisib and inavolisib). **E**

> **9.35b** Use of insulin should be reserved for **severe hyperglycemia and hyperglycemic crises** due to its potential impact on the efficacy of PI3K inhibitors. **E**

> **9.36** Adjust or initiate additional glucose-lowering therapies to maintain individualized glycemic goals based on the specific glucocorticoid treatment plan, with frequent reassessment of glucose levels and glucocorticoid treatment plans. **C**

> **9.37** In adults with posttransplantation diabetes mellitus (PTDM) or preexisting type 2 diabetes, insulin is preferred for the management of hyperglycemia in the postoperative setting. **A** A DPP-4 inhibitor can be considered for mild hyperglycemia. **A**

> ⚠⚠ **Rec 9.35b 極重要，且必須完整引用。**
> ADA 的原文是 insulin 「**should be reserved for severe hyperglycemia and hyperglycemic crises**」——亦即 ADA **明白保留了嚴重高血糖／高血糖危症使用 insulin 的空間**，而非全面禁用。
> 這正是本回顧禁忌第 3 條所指：**不可因理論上想避免 hyperinsulinemia，而延誤嚴重高血糖／DKA／HHS 所需的 insulin。** ADA 自己的條文就已內建這個例外。撰寫時**不得**把 9.35b 簡化成「PI3Ki 病人不能打 insulin」。
> 另注意 9.35b 的證據等級僅為 **E（expert consensus）**，其「potential impact on the efficacy」屬機轉推論【L5】層級，非臨床結果證據。

## 2. capivasertib (TRUQAP) — **AKT inhibitor，僅作比較用** [S5]

> ⚠ **分類警告**：capivasertib 是 **pan-AKT inhibitor（AKT1/2/3）**，**不是** PI3Kα inhibitor。它作用於 PI3K–AKT–mTOR 路徑的**下游**節點。以下所有數字**只能**用於「比較」，**不得**移植到 alpelisib 或 inavolisib 的臨床決策上。

### 2.1 標籤基本資料
> TRUQAP® (capivasertib) tablets, for oral use — Initial U.S. Approval: 2023

> The recommended dosage of TRUQAP, in combination with abiraterone and prednisone, is 400 mg orally twice daily (approximately 12 hours apart) with or without food, **for 4 days followed by 3 days off**. Continue TRUQAP until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

> ⚠ **間歇給藥（4 天 on／3 天 off）是 capivasertib 的核心設計**，與 alpelisib 的**每日連續**給藥根本不同。這直接影響高血糖的時間曲線與監測邏輯（見下方「on days and off days」的驗糖指示），**不可類推**。

### 2.2 治療前評估（逐字）
> **2.2 Recommended Evaluation Before Initiating TRUQAP**
> Evaluate fasting blood glucose (FG) and hemoglobin A1C (HbA1C) prior to starting TRUQAP and at regular intervals during treatment see [Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

### 2.3 §5.1 Hyperglycemia（Warnings and Precautions，逐字）
> TRUQAP can cause severe hyperglycemia, including diabetic ketoacidosis and fatal outcomes.

> After initiating treatment with TRUQAP, monitor or self-monitor fasting glucose levels on **Day 3 or 4 of the dosing week during weeks 1, 2, 4, 6 and 8; then monthly** while on treatment with TRUQAP; and as clinically indicated. Monitor HbA1C levels **every 3 months** during treatment with TRUQAP and as clinically indicated and manage changes as appropriate. Patients with a history of well-controlled Type 2 diabetes mellitus may require intensified anti-hyperglycemic treatment and require close monitoring of fasting glucose levels.

> Withhold TRUQAP in clinical situations known to increase the risk of severe hyperglycemia or ketoacidosis (e.g., **suspected serious infection or acute illness**).

> Withhold TRUQAP immediately when ketoacidosis is suspected. If ketoacidosis is confirmed, **permanently discontinue TRUQAP**.

> The safety of TRUQAP has not been established in patients with Type 1 diabetes or Type 2 diabetes that is **uncontrolled or requiring insulin at baseline** as these patients were **excluded from clinical studies** see Clinical Studies ([14.1,14.2)].

> Based on the severity of the hyperglycemia, withhold, reduce dose, or permanently discontinue TRUQAP see [Dosage and Administration (2.5)].

> ⚠ 對癌症病人**脫水／急性病／感染**的情境，TRUQAP 仿單有**明文的停藥指示**（"suspected serious infection or acute illness"）。這是本回顧「須顧及脫水與腎功能波動」的**唯一在本檔中有仿單級【L1】依據**的條文——但它是 **capivasertib 的仿單**，**不是** alpelisib 的。

| **FG 251‑500 mg/dL**<br>or **FG 14‑27.8 mmol/L** | Withhold TRUQAP until FG decrease ≤ 160 mg/dL (or ≤ 8.9 mmol/L).<br>If recovery occurs in **≤ 28 days**, resume TRUQAP at **one lower dose**.<br>If recovery occurs in **> 28 days**, **permanently discontinue** TRUQAP. |

> 額外指示（逐字）：For patients who experience hyperglycemia during treatment with TRUQAP, monitor FG **at least twice weekly, on days on and off TRUQAP**, until FG decreases to baseline levels.

### 2.5 CAPItello-291（乳癌）高血糖數據（逐字）[S5]

> **CAPItello 291**
> Increased fasting glucose from baseline occurred in **37%** of patients treated with TRUQAP, including **11%** of patients with **Grade 2 (FG > 160 to 250 mg/dL)**, **2%** with **Grade 3 (FG > 250 to 500 mg/dL)**, and **1.1%** with **Grade 4 (FG > 500 mg/dL)** events.

> The median time to first occurrence of hyperglycemia was **15 days (range: 1 to 367)**. Dose reduction for hyperglycemia was required in **0.6%** of patients and permanent discontinuation was required in **0.6%** of patients.

> **12% (43/355)** of patients who received TRUQAP had an anti-hyperglycemic medication regimen either initiated or changed during the study, including treatment with **insulin in 4.8% (17/355)** of patients.

> Diabetic metabolic decompensation occurred in **0.6%** of patients, including **diabetic ketoacidosis in 0.3%**.

### 2.6 CAPItello-281（攝護腺癌，併用 abiraterone + prednisone）高血糖數據（逐字）[S5]

> Increased fasting glucose from baseline occurred in **69%** of patients treated with TRUQAP, including **25%** of patients with Grade 2 (FG > 160 to 250 mg/dL), **12%** with Grade 3 (FG > 250 to 500 mg/dL), and **1.2%** of patients with Grade 4 (FG > 500 mg/dL) events.

> The median time to first occurrence of hyperglycemia was **71 days (range: 1 to 1454)**. Dose reduction for hyperglycemia was required in **8.7%** of patients and permanent discontinuation was required in **2.4%** of patients.

> **41% (204/503)** of patients who received TRUQAP had an anti-hyperglycemic medication regimen either initiated or changed during the study, including treatment with **insulin in 16% (81/503)** of patients.

> Diabetic ketoacidosis occurred in **1.2%** of patients.

> ⚠⚠ **CAPItello-291 vs CAPItello-281 差異巨大（37% vs 69%；DKA 0.3% vs 1.2%；median onset 15 天 vs 71 天）。**
> 兩者的差別包含**併用 prednisone**（281 為 capivasertib + abiraterone + **prednisone**）與不同癌別族群。
> **不可**把 capivasertib 的高血糖風險當成單一數字陳述；**必須**指明是哪一個 trial、哪一種 backbone。
> 這也再次示範 ADA Rec 3.9（併用高劑量類固醇者的高血糖風險）的臨床相關性。

## 3. 其他 PI3K inhibitors 的高血糖特性比較

> ⚠ **本節的核心結論：高血糖並非 PI3K inhibitor 的一致 class effect，而是與 α isoform 抑制的程度高度相關。**
> 這與 ADA 只針對「PI3K inhibitors **that affect the α isoform**」發布建議（Rec 9.35a）的做法一致。

### 3.1 copanlisib (ALIQOPA) — **pan-PI3K（含 α），IV，已於美國撤照**

**§5.2 Hyperglycemia（逐字）[S6]**
> Grade 3 or 4 hyperglycemia (blood glucose 250 mg/dL or greater) occurred in **41% of 317 patients** treated with ALIQOPA monotherapy [see Adverse Reactions (6.1)]. **Serious hyperglycemic events occurred in 2.8% of patients.** Treatment with ALIQOPA may result in **infusion-related hyperglycemia**. Blood glucose levels typically **peaked 5 to 8 hours post-infusion** and subsequently declined to baseline levels for a majority of patients; blood glucose levels **remained elevated in 17.7% of patients one day after ALIQOPA infusion**. Of **155 patients with baseline HbA1c <5.7%, 16 (10%) patients had HbA1c >6.5% at the end of treatment**.

**Highlights 摘要（逐字）[S6]**
> Hyperglycemia: **Start each infusion once optimal blood glucose control is achieved.** Withhold treatment, reduce dose, or discontinue treatment depending on the severity and persistence of hyperglycemia (5.2).

**劑量調整表 hyperglycemia 列（逐字，完整 2 列）[S6]**

| Severity（逐字） | Action（逐字） |
|---|---|
| Pre-dose **fasting** blood glucose **160 mg/dL or more** or **random/non-fasting** blood glucose of **200 mg/dL or more** | Withhold ALIQOPA until fasting glucose is 160 mg/dL or less, or a random/non-fasting blood glucose of 200 mg/dL or less. |
| **Pre-dose or post-dose blood glucose 500 mg/dL or more** | On first occurrence, withhold ALIQOPA until fasting blood glucose is 160 mg/dL or less, or a random/non-fasting blood glucose of 200 mg/dL or less. Then **reduce ALIQOPA from 60 mg to 45 mg and maintain**. |

**上市狀態（重要）[S7]**
copanlisib 於 2017-09 依加速核准（accelerated approval）取得美國 relapsed follicular lymphoma 適應症；因 CHRONOS-4 確認性試驗未達 PFS 主要指標，Bayer 於 2023-12-08 提出撤照申請，FDA 於 **2024-03-18** 正式撤銷 NDA 核准（Federal Register 2024-05619）。
> ⚠ 撰寫回顧時**必須**註明 copanlisib 目前**已非美國在售藥品**，其數據僅具歷史／機轉比較價值。

**臨床型態對比**：copanlisib 的高血糖是**輸注相關、短暫、峰值在 infusion 後 5–8 小時**，與 alpelisib／inavolisib 的**口服每日、持續性**高血糖在時間動力學上**完全不同**。兩者不可共用同一套監測或處置流程。

### 3.2 idelalisib (ZYDELIG) — **PI3Kδ 選擇性**

**2014 初版標籤 Highlights（逐字）[S8]**
> The most common laboratory abnormalities (incidence ≥30%) are neutropenia, hypertriglyceridemia, **hyperglycemia**, ALT elevations, and AST elevations (6.1).

**完整數字（ZYDELIG Canadian Product Monograph, Table 3；CLL, Study 312-0116）[S10]**
> triglycerides (hypertriglyceridemia) 62 (56) 3 (3) 37 (34) 0
> **hyperglycemia 59 (54) 8 (7) 50 (46) 2 (2)**
> **hypoglycemia 12 (11) 0 5 (5) 0**
> hyponatremia 22 (20) 2 (2) 16 (15) 7 (6)
> （欄位為：ZYDELIG + R, N=110 — Any Grade / Grade 3–4；Placebo + R, N=108 — Any Grade / Grade 3–4）

| 參數 | ZYDELIG+R 任何等級 | ZYDELIG+R Grade 3–4 | Placebo+R 任何等級 | Placebo+R Grade 3–4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hyperglycemia** | 59 (**54%**) | 8 (**7%**) | 50 (**46%**) | 2 (**2%**) |
| Hypoglycemia | 12 (11%) | 0 | 5 (5%) | 0 |

> ⚠⚠ **這一列是本節最重要的鑑別點：安慰劑組的 hyperglycemia 已達 46%。**
> idelalisib 組 54% vs 安慰劑 46%，**組間差距僅約 8 個百分點**；Grade 3–4 為 7% vs 2%。
> 也就是說，δ 選擇性的 idelalisib **並未**產生 alpelisib 等級的高血糖訊號，且其表列數字大部分可由背景族群與合併治療解釋。
> **不可**引用「idelalisib 高血糖 54%」而不同時引用安慰劑組的 46%——那會嚴重誤導。

**現行標籤的變化 [S9]**
現行 US PI（2018 s009 起）之 Table 3 已改題為 "**Hematologic and Hepatic** Laboratory Abnormalities"，僅列 neutropenia、leukopenia、lymphocytopenia、ALT increased、AST increased —— **hyperglycemia 已不在表列**。現行 Highlights 亦僅載：
> Common laboratory abnormalities were neutropenia, ALT elevations and AST elevations.

**W&P 與 boxed warning**：本次擷取於 ZYDELIG 之 Warnings and Precautions 與 boxed warning **未見** hyperglycemia 條目（boxed warning 內容為 hepatotoxicity、diarrhea/colitis、pneumonitis、infections、intestinal perforation 等）。EMA Product Information 的 ADR table [S13] 中，代謝相關僅列 "Increased triglycerides — Very common / Common"，**未列** hyperglycemia。

### 3.3 duvelisib (COPIKTRA) — **PI3Kδ/γ 選擇性**

**W&P（逐字）[S11]**
> WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS
> • Treatment-related mortality occurred in 15% of COPIKTRA treated patients.
> • Fatal and/or serious infections occurred in 31% (4% fatal) of COPIKTRA-treated patients...
> • Fatal and/or serious diarrhea or colitis occurred in 18% (<1% fatal)...
> • Fatal and/or serious cutaneous reactions occurred in 5% (<1% fatal)...
> • Fatal and/or serious pneumonitis occurred in 5% (<1% fatal)...

**常見不良反應（逐字）[S11]**
> The most common adverse reactions (reported in ≥ 20% of patients) were **diarrhea or colitis, neutropenia, rash, fatigue, pyrexia, cough, nausea, upper respiratory infection, pneumonia, musculoskeletal pain, and anemia.**

> Grade 4 adverse reactions occurring in ≥ 2% of recipients of COPIKTRA included neutropenia (18%), thrombocytopenia (6%), sepsis (3%), **hypokalemia** and increased lipase (2% each), and pneumonia and pneumonitis (2% each).

**結論**：COPIKTRA 之 4 項 boxed warning 與 W&P **均不含 hyperglycemia**；≥20% 常見 AE 清單中**亦無 hyperglycemia**。
> ⚠ 本次擷取**未**取得 COPIKTRA Table 5／Table 7（laboratory abnormalities）的完整逐列數字，因此**不得**斷言「duvelisib 完全不引起任何程度的血糖上升」。可安全陳述的僅為：**hyperglycemia 未被列為 boxed warning、W&P 或 ≥20% 常見不良反應**。

**可安全下的論斷（**僅限本檔已列證據**）：**
1. 顯著、需要積極處置的高血糖，**集中在會抑制 α isoform 的藥物**（alpelisib、inavolisib、copanlisib）與下游的 AKT inhibitor（capivasertib）。
2. **δ／δγ 選擇性藥物（idelalisib、duvelisib）的仿單均無 hyperglycemia 之 W&P**，且 idelalisib 的表列數字與安慰劑差距甚小。
3. 因此「PI3K inhibitor 都會造成高血糖」是**錯誤的 class 概化**。ADA 用詞（"PI3K inhibitors that affect the α isoform"）也支持這一點。
