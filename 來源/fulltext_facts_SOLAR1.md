## 0. 族群與分母定義（引用數字時必須對上）

- Safety population = 至少接受一劑者，共 571 人：alpelisib 組 **284**、placebo 組 **287**（1 名 placebo 組收案但未給藥）。
- 隨機分派（enrolled）為 alpelisib 284 / placebo 288。
- PIK3CA-mutant 341 人、non-mutant 231 人；安全性分析將兩個 cohort 合併。

> The safety population comprised 571 patients, with 284 patients in the alpelisib group and 287 in the placebo group (1 patient in the placebo group was enrolled but did not receive study treatment).
（Results, *Patient characteristics*，line 208-210）

> Data from patients with *PIK3CA*-mutant and non-mutant disease were combined, since no significant difference in safety profile existed between the alpelisib and placebo group across the two *PIK3CA* cohorts [9].
（Methods, *Statistical analysis*，line 179-181）

**基線特徵**：alpelisib 組中位年齡 62 歲、placebo 組 64 歲；約 86% 有 endocrine resistance；49% 有肺／肝轉移；約 6% 曾用 CDK4/6 inhibitor。
> median ages were 62 and 64 years in the alpelisib and placebo groups, respectively; in both treatment groups, approximately 86% of patients had endocrine resistance (per protocol definition), 49% of patients had lung or liver metastases, and approximately 6% of patients had previously received CDK4/6 inhibitors [9].（line 201-205）

**收案資格**：允許 well-controlled T2DM；排除 T1DM 與 uncontrolled T2DM。
> Patients with a history of well-controlled type 2 diabetes were eligible to enroll; however, patients with type 1 and uncontrolled type 2 diabetes were excluded.（Methods，line 123-125）

**Alpelisib 劑量與減量階梯**：300 mg/day with food；可階梯式減量至 250、200 mg/day。
> Stepwise dose reductions of alpelisib or placebo were permitted (300 mg/day, 250 mg/day, 200 mg/day) to manage AEs.（line 127-128）
> Starting dose: 300 mg/day continuously. Dose level −1: 250 mg/day continuously. Dose level −2: 200 mg/day continuously.（Table 1 註 b，line 800）

**血糖監測頻率**：FPG 於篩選、前 8 週每 2 週、之後每 4 週；且第 1-4 週另加 day 8、day 15。
> Vital signs and hematological and biochemical laboratory parameters were assessed at screening, every 2 weeks for the first 8 weeks, and then every 4 weeks. Fasting plasma glucose (FPG) was also assessed on days 8 and 15 in the first 4 weeks.（Methods, *Safety assessments*，line 137-139）

（Results, *Time to onset and improvement of AESIs*，line 221-224）

## 3. 高血糖的處置：降糖藥使用比率

- 187 名 any-grade 高血糖（grouped term）者中，**163 人**接受降糖藥物。
- 最常用者為 **metformin，87.1%**（單用或合併）。
- **67 人（41.1%）**僅需一種降糖藥；**47 人（28.8%）**需要三種以上。

> Of the 187 patients experiencing any-grade hyperglycemia by grouped term in the alpelisib cohort, 163 received medication to manage this event. The most frequently used antidiabetic medication was metformin (87.1%) administered either alone or in combination with other agents. In 67 patients (41.1%), only one antidiabetic medication was required to manage hyperglycemia, whereas in 47 patients (28.8%), three or more medications were required (**Supplemental Table 4**).
（Results, *Supportive medications to manage hyperglycemia*，line 252-257）

> 註：87.1%、41.1%、28.8% 的分母為 **163 名接受降糖藥者**，非 284 亦非 187。

### 3-1 Insulin 使用（依基線血糖狀態）

- Diabetic 者：**5 / 12**
- Prediabetic 者：**34 / 159**
- Normal 者：**13 / 113**
- 合計 **52 人**用過 insulin：**33 人**為長期使用（>2 天），**19 人**為 rescue 用藥。

> During the study, insulin was used by 5 of 12 patients with diabetes, 34 of 159 patients with prediabetes, and 13 of 113 patients with normal glycemic status at baseline. Insulin use may have been in combination with other antidiabetic mediations. Of the 52 patients receiving insulin, 33 received it as long-term treatment (> 2 days), while 19 received it as rescue medication.
（line 257-261）

### 3-2 作者對藥物選擇的立場（討論段，屬作者論述而非試驗結果）

> It is noteworthy that insulin sensitizers (e.g., metformin) may be preferable to insulin secretagogues (e.g., sulfonylurea, meglitinides) to manage hyperglycemia in patients treated with alpelisib due to the insulin spikes and relative resistance noted with PI3K inhibitors [5,10]. Beyond metformin, there is no second agent widely accepted as a standard to treat hyperglycemia due to PI3K inhibitors. Some consider sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 (SGLT2) inhibitors to be the best choice, however, more data is needed to support their use [10].
（Discussion，line 368-374）

> **However, short-term insulin is clearly effective for managing acute cases as well as more severe hyperglycemia associated with alpelisib and not controlled by oral antihyperglycemic medications alone.**
（Discussion，line 374-377）
→ 本文明確支持：嚴重／急性高血糖不應迴避 insulin。

|---|---|---|---|

## 7. Protocol amendment 的影響

**修訂時機與內容**：於約 560 名計畫收案數中已隨機 **317 人（56.6%）**時修訂；HbA1c 收案門檻由 **< 8% 改為 < 6.5%**；對基線 FPG ≥ 100 mg/dL (5.6 mmol/L) 且／或 HbA1c ≥ 5.7% 者建議 screening 時衛教生活型態調整並轉介專科；新增 **day 8 門診**以早期偵測高血糖與皮膚毒性；建議 rash 發生前先用口服抗組織胺。

> The study protocol was amended to improve monitoring and management of hyperglycemia and skin toxicity after 317 (56.6%) of approximately 560 planned patients had been randomized to study treatment. The amendments were introduced based on recommendations by an advisory board of experts in managing these AESIs. At the start of the study, the HbA1c criterion for inclusion was < 8%, which was then modified to < 6.5%, excluding patients with uncontrolled diabetes. Instruction on lifestyle changes at screening and consultation with a healthcare specialist were recommended for patients with baseline FPG ≥ 100 mg/dL (5.6 mmol/L) and/or HbA1c ≥ 5.7%.
（Methods, *On-study management of AESI*，line 162-169）

> The amendment updated the eligibility criteria to include only patients with an HbA1c < 6.5%, recommended the use of oral antihistamines prior to the onset of rash, and added a clinic visit at day 8 to identify hyperglycemia and skin toxicities earlier.
（Results，line 317-319）

**比較方式**：前 50% 隨機者 vs 後 50% 隨機者（非嚴格 amendment 前後）。

> To investigate the impact of the amendment, treatment discontinuation rate and median duration of exposure were compared between the first 50% of patients randomized and the last 50%.（line 319-322）

|---|---|---|
| 高血糖 any grade（preferred term） | 63.9% | 63.6% |
| 高血糖 grade 3/4 | **40.3%** | **32.9%** |
| 因高血糖停藥 | **9.0%** | **3.6%** |
| 因 grade 3/4 高血糖停藥（Figure 4） | 5.6% | 2.9% |
| Rash any grade | 37.5% | 33.6% |
| Rash grade 3/4 | 11.1% | 8.6% |
| Diarrhea any grade（Figure 4） | 62.5% | 52.9% |
| Diarrhea grade 3/4（Figure 4） | 8.3% | 5.0% |
| 因任何級 AE 停藥（alpelisib 或 placebo） | **29.2%** | **20.7%** |
| 因 grade ≥3 AE 停藥 | **18.1%** | **7.9%** |

> All-grade hyperglycemia (preferred term) was reported in 63.9% of the first 50% of patients randomized and 63.6% in the last 50%, while grade 3/4 was reported in 40.3% and 32.9%, respectively. All-grade rash (preferred term) was reported in 37.5% and 33.6%, respectively, and grade 3/4 was 11.1% and 8.6%. Discontinuation of alpelisib or placebo due to AEs was less frequent in the last 50% of patients randomized compared with the first 50%: discontinuation rate due to any-grade AEs was 20.7% vs 29.2%, and discontinuation rate due to grade ≥ 3 AEs was 7.9% vs 18.1%, respectively. （Results，line 322-330）
