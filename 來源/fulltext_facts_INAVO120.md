## 0. 試驗設計與劑量（背景）

**事實**：phase 3、double-blind、randomized、placebo-controlled；inavolisib 9 mg PO QD（28 天週期 D1–28）+ palbociclib 125 mg PO QD（D1–21）+ fulvestrant 500 mg IM（C1 D1、D15，之後每約 28 天）。28 國收案。

> "Patients were randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio to receive inavolisib (at a dose of 9 mg, administered orally, once daily on days 1 to 28 of each 28-day cycle) or placebo (once daily), each given with palbociclib (at a dose of 125 mg, administered orally, once daily on days 1 to 21 of each 28-day cycle) and fulvestrant (at a dose of 500 mg, administered intramuscularly, on days 1 and 15 of cycle 1 and approximately every 28 days thereafter)."
> — METHODS, TRIAL DESIGN AND TREATMENT（p.1586）

> "The INAVO120 trial enrolled patients in 28 countries."
> — METHODS, TRIAL OVERSIGHT（p.1585）

**收案期間與追蹤**：

> "Between January 29, 2020, and September 14, 2023, we enrolled 325 patients."
> "The median follow-up was 21.3 months in the inavolisib group and 21.5 months in the placebo group (data cutoff, September 29, 2023)."
> — RESULTS, PATIENTS（p.1587）

## 1. 納入／排除條件中的**血糖門檻**（Q4 cross-trial 論證關鍵）

### 1.1 納入門檻（逐字）

**事實**：fasting glucose **< 126 mg/dL**；HbA1c **< 6.0%**。

> "Additional eligibility criteria included disease recurrence or progression during or within 12 months after the completion of adjuvant endocrine therapy (patients with de novo metastatic breast cancer were excluded), **a fasting glucose level of less than 126 mg per deciliter, a glycated hemoglobin level of less than 6.0%**, and measurable disease according to the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST), version 1.1."
> — METHODS, PATIENTS（p.1586）

單位註記：原文為 **mg per deciliter**（mg/dL），未提供 mmol/L 換算值。

### 1.2 糖尿病排除條件（逐字）

**事實**：**需要持續治療的 type 1 或 type 2 diabetes 病人被排除**；作者自列為試驗限制之一。

> "Third, **patients with type 1 or type 2 diabetes that required ongoing treatment were excluded**; thus, future studies that evaluate the benefit–risk profile in this population will be useful."
> — DISCUSSION, limitations（p.1594）

**臨床推論邊界（本文件作者註）**：原文只寫「需要持續治療的糖尿病」被排除，
並未逐字說明「未用藥的 diet-controlled DM」是否可入組；本回顧不對此作斷言。

### 3.3 依 BMI 分層的高血糖發生率

**事實**：inavolisib 組中 BMI ≥30.0 者 hyperglycemia 發生率 **65.5%**，BMI <30.0 者 **56.8%**。

> "The incidence of hyperglycemia was **65.5%** among patients in the inavolisib group with a body-mass index (BMI; the weight in kilograms divided by the square of the height in meters) of at least 30.0 and **56.8%** among those with a BMI of less than 30.0."
> — RESULTS, SAFETY（p.1589）

> "The percentage of patients with hyperglycemia was higher in the inavolisib group than in the placebo group, and the incidence of hyperglycemia was **slightly higher** among patients with a BMI of at least 30.0 than among those with a BMI of less than 30.0; both findings can be attributed to the fact that **hyperglycemia is an on-target toxic effect associated with PI3K pathway inhibitors**."
> — DISCUSSION（p.1592）

註：此 BMI 分層僅報 any-grade，**未報 BMI 分層下的 grade 3/4 率**。

## 4. 高血糖之處置、減量、中斷、停藥

### 4.1 因高血糖減量

**事實**：hyperglycemia 導致 inavolisib **減量者佔 2.5%**，且是唯一達 ≥2% 的減量原因。
整體任何 AE 導致 inavolisib／placebo 減量分別為 **14.2%** 與 **3.1%**。

> "Adverse events led to a reduction in the dose of inavolisib and placebo in **14.2%** and **3.1%** of the patients, respectively. **Hyperglycemia led to a reduction in the inavolisib dose in 2.5% of the patients; this was the only adverse event that led to a reduction in the inavolisib dose in at least 2% of the patients.**"
> — RESULTS, SAFETY（p.1591）

### 4.2 停藥

**事實**：任一試驗藥物因 AE 停藥 inavolisib 組 **6.8%** vs placebo 組 **0.6%**；
分項：inavolisib 6.2%、palbociclib 4.9%、fulvestrant 3.1%。

> "Adverse events led to the discontinuation of any trial agent in **6.8%** of the patients in the inavolisib group (**6.2%** of the patients discontinued inavolisib; **4.9%**, palbociclib; and **3.1%**, fulvestrant) and in **0.6%** of those in the placebo group (no patients discontinued palbociclib or fulvestrant because of adverse events) (Table S3)."
> — RESULTS, SAFETY（p.1591）

**未報告**：**因 hyperglycemia 而停藥的比率**（原文未單獨列出；相關細節在 Table S3 補充附錄，本地全文檔不含 Supplementary Appendix）。

### 4.3 處置方式與 metformin 預防性使用

**事實**：protocol **允許**高風險病人**預防性使用 metformin**；原文以「supportive care 與 dose modification 可控制」描述整體處置。

> "However, **hyperglycemia, diarrhea, stomatitis, and rash were controlled with supportive care and dose modifications**, which is reflected in the high dose intensity of each trial drug. **The protocol allowed prophylactic use of metformin in patients with a high risk of hyperglycemia** and recommended early use of dexamethasone mouthwash as treatment or prophylaxis for stomatitis on the basis of results of the SWISH study."
> — DISCUSSION（p.1592）

**未報告（極重要）**：
- **實際接受 metformin（預防性或治療性）的病人比率**：原文**未報告任何百分比**。
- **「高風險」的操作型定義**（依 BMI？依 FPG？依 HbA1c？）：原文未定義。
- **降糖藥種類（SGLT2i／insulin／SU 等）之使用**：原文未報告。
- **高血糖之具體處置演算法（何時暫停、何時減量至 6 mg／3 mg）**：正文未載，僅稱「described in the protocol」。

> "Dose modifications for inavolisib and palbociclib are described in the protocol; dose reductions for fulvestrant were not allowed."
> — METHODS, TRIAL DESIGN AND TREATMENT（p.1586）

### 4.4 中位發生時間

**原文未報告** hyperglycemia 的 median time to onset、median time to resolution、
或首次發生的週期分佈。全文無 "time to onset" 相關敘述。
（對照組：此類時序資料見 SOLAR-1 的 `原始PDF/SOLAR1_AE_Rugo_2020.md`，不可移植至 inavolisib。）

## 5. 劑量強度（間接反映毒性可控程度）

**事實**：inavolisib 組中位服藥時間 inavolisib 9.2 個月、palbociclib 9.1 個月、fulvestrant 8.6 個月；
中位相對劑量強度分別為 **95.8%**、**87.3%**、**100.0%**。
Placebo 組中位服藥 5.6 個月，palbociclib 相對劑量強度 **88.4%**、fulvestrant **100.0%**。

> "In the safety analysis population (162 patients in each trial group), patients in the inavolisib group received inavolisib for a median of 9.2 months; palbociclib, for 9.1 months; and fulvestrant, for 8.6 months. The median relative dose intensities were 95.8%, 87.3%, and 100.0%, respectively."
> "Patients in the placebo group received placebo for a median of 5.6 months; palbociclib, for 5.6 months; and fulvestrant, for 5.6 months. The median relative dose intensities were 88.4% for palbociclib and 100.0% for fulvestrant."
> — RESULTS, TREATMENT（p.1587）

**關於 palbociclib 減量與高血糖是否交互影響**：
**原文未報告**任何 palbociclib 劑量調整與 hyperglycemia 之交互分析。
可 grep 到的僅有：palbociclib 相對劑量強度 87.3%（inavolisib 組）vs 88.4%（placebo 組）——
兩組接近，且 palbociclib 減量的主要驅動因子（neutropenia）在兩組發生率相近
（grade 3/4 neutropenia 80.2% vs 78.4%）。原文**未**把 palbociclib 減量歸因於高血糖。

## 6. 試驗族群的特殊性（cross-trial 比較的關鍵警訊）

### 6.1 **並非** CDK4/6i 治療中／治療後進展族群

**事實**：INAVO120 要求的是**輔助內分泌治療期間或完成後 12 個月內**復發（一線治療設定），
**不是** CDK4/6i 進展後。**98.8% 病人未曾接受過 CDK4/6 抑制劑**
（曾用 neoadjuvant/adjuvant CDK4/6i 者僅 4/325 = 1.2%）。De novo 轉移病人被排除。

> "…patients with *PIK3CA*-mutated, hormone receptor–positive, human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2)–negative locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer who **had had relapse during or within 12 months after the completion of adjuvant endocrine therapy**."
> — ABSTRACT, METHODS（p.1584）

> "Most of the patients had previously received neoadjuvant or adjuvant chemotherapy (82.8%) and **had not previously received a CDK4/6 inhibitor (98.8%)**, and 47.7% of the patients had previously received neoadjuvant or adjuvant tamoxifen only."
> — RESULTS, PATIENTS（p.1587）

Table 1：Previous neoadjuvant or adjuvant CDK4/6 inhibitor — 3 (1.9%) / 1 (0.6%) / **4 (1.2%)**（p.1589）

> "Second, **few patients had previously received adjuvant CDK4/6 inhibitors**, given that recruitment primarily occurred before adjuvant CDK4/6 inhibitors were available."
> — DISCUSSION, limitations（p.1594）

**⚠️ 稽核提醒**：任何把 INAVO120 描述成「CDK4/6i 治療中或治療後短期內進展族群」的敘述
與原文不符。原文的 enrichment 條件是**內分泌治療抗性**（primary/secondary resistance），非 CDK4/6i 進展。

### 6.2 內分泌治療抗性定義（分層因子）

> "Primary resistance to endocrine therapy was defined as **relapse during the first 2 years of adjuvant endocrine therapy**, and secondary resistance to endocrine therapy was defined as **relapse after the start of year 2 of adjuvant endocrine therapy or relapse within 12 months after the completion of adjuvant endocrine therapy**."
> — METHODS, TRIAL DESIGN AND TREATMENT（p.1586）

Primary resistance 111 (34.2%)、Secondary resistance 213 (65.5%)、missing 1 (0.3%)（Table 1, p.1588）

### 6.3 高腫瘤負荷 enrichment

> "The trial population was enriched for patients with poor prognostic factors. We enrolled patients with resistance to endocrine therapy and measurable disease, which resulted in a large percentage of patients with a high disease burden, including **metastases in at least 3 organs (in 51.4% of the patients), visceral disease (in 80.0%), and liver metastases (in 51.7%)**."
> — DISCUSSION（p.1592）

**臨床意涵（本文件作者註）**：高內臟／肝轉移負荷、且 47.1% BMI 正常、5.5% BMI <18.5，
代表此族群本身有食慾下降（decreased appetite any grade 23.5%）與腹瀉（48.1%）風險，
血糖管理時須同時顧及脫水與攝食不足。

## 9. 原文對 alpelisib 的直接比較敘述（cross-trial，須謹慎）

**事實**：原文引用 SOLAR-1（ref 11）與 BOLERO-2（ref 13）之停藥率作對照，
並**自行加註 cross-trial 比較須謹慎**。

> "For example, treatment discontinuation because of adverse events occurred in **25.0%** of the patients who received combination therapy with alpelisib and in **19%** of those who received combination therapy with everolimus, whereas treatment discontinuation because of adverse events occurred in **6.8%** of the patients in the inavolisib group in our trial. **Cross-trial comparisons should be made with caution owing to differences in trial design, patient populations, and analysis and reporting methods.**"

## 11. 對本回顧論證的三點提醒

1. **不可與 alpelisib 混談**：INAVO120 的 grade 3/4 hyperglycemia 5.6%（n=9/162）
   是在「FPG <126 mg/dL、HbA1c <6.0%、排除需治療糖尿病、中位體重 63 kg、BMI ≥30 僅 17.5%」
   的族群中取得。此數字**不能**外推至真實世界的糖尿病或肥胖病人。
2. **不可稱 metformin 已被證實可預防**：原文僅寫 protocol *allowed* prophylactic metformin，
   **未報告使用率、未做隨機化、未報告其對高血糖發生率的影響**。任何「預防性 metformin 有效」的
   斷言在 INAVO120 全文中**無可驗證來源**。
3. **不可稱 INAVO120 是 CDK4/6i 後線族群**：98.8% 為 CDK4/6i-naive，屬**一線**治療設定。
